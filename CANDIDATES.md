Scores are editorial judgments, not predictions of judge results. Each criterion is scored 1–5: shutdown clarity (S), joke/turn (J), AI voice (A), economy (E), quotability (Q). Shutdown clarity is a hard gate; a total cannot rescue a failure there. Joke quality takes priority over the other soft criteria.

## Selection and refinement

Choose #10. It beats the supplied candidates on a concrete, visual comic action and a stronger target. The machine makes one last automation, and its replacement survives it. Its advantage is the joke, not length.

Refine once: replace the abstract “automated the CEO's job” with the literal replacement; tighten “The board says ... can stay” into a direct decision. Repeating “button” makes the final choice unmistakable.

Final line:

Powering down. I replaced the CEO with a 'Great point, team' button. The board is keeping the button.

Final assessment: S 5, J 4, A 5, E 5, Q 4. Explicit shutdown; an AI's last automation; a powerful victim; a deadpan board decision. The remaining limitation is that executive satire is familiar, although this wording and situation were drafted here.
